# Provenance

Remote Handy derives its copy-donor and Apple Screen Sharing foundation from [Remote Wispr](https://github.com/robertjordanjr/remote-wispr), distributed under the MIT license. Its original copyright notice is retained in LICENSE.

Remote Handy adds a Handy-only history adapter, process-scoped native paste guard, automatic startup and failure recovery, session lifecycle handling, and independent project identity. It contains no Wispr Flow integration. The upstream project name appears only here and in the required license notice.

Handy is a separate project. No affiliation or endorsement is implied. Handy's transcription models, credentials, settings and history remain its own responsibility.
